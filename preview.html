Research

Pioneering AI safety

We conduct frontier research to build AI systems that are safe, beneficial, and understandable. Our work spans interpretability, alignment, and the societal impacts of AI.

Interpretability

Understanding what happens inside AI models — mapping the circuits and features that drive behavior.

Alignment

Ensuring AI systems reliably follow human intent and values, even as capabilities grow.

Responsible Scaling

A framework for safely developing increasingly capable AI systems with appropriate safeguards.

Societal Impacts

Studying how AI affects economics, labor markets, and society to inform responsible deployment.

Our Commitments

Building AI responsibly

We believe AI safety isn't just a research problem — it's a commitment to how we build, deploy, and govern AI systems.

01

Safety First

Every model we release undergoes rigorous safety testing. We develop and publish our Responsible Scaling Policy to guide decisions.

02

Transparency

We share our research openly, publish model cards and system prompts, and engage with policymakers and civil society.

03

Broad Benefit

We work to ensure AI benefits are widely shared, investing in education, economic research, and global access.
